Tally — a mark made.

Five concepts built around the obvious: a tally mark is the most literal possible logo for a session-tracking app. Each is shown in light + dark, on its own and inside a wordmark. Pick a direction — I'll cut a final SVG, app icons, and favicon.

01 the concepts

Concept A is the recommended direction — it's literal, scales, works at 16px, and the diagonal stroke is a crown wink.

A · Five-bar tallyRECOMMENDED
Tally

Concept: the universal "5" tally — four bars and a slash. The slash is the brand yellow. Reads as a board-game scoreboard at a glance, and the diagonal almost forms a crown when squinted at. Best at 16px because the bars stay distinct.

B · Dice "T"
Tally

Concept: dice pips arranged as the letter T. Playful, immediately reads "board game". Slightly riskier at small sizes — pips can mush at 16×16.

C · Crown + tally
Tally

Concept: tally marks topped by a crown — connects to the leaderboard / "champion" surface. Richer story but busier; smallest size suffers.

D · Wordmark + slash
Tall y
Tally — wordmark only

Concept: just the word, struck through by a single tally slash. Most "designy", least friendly as an app icon. Pairs well with concept A as icon.
